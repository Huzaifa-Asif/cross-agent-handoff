# Gemini to Grok

1. Export or select Gemini documents/conversations you are authorized to move.
2. Choose **Full Migration**, source **Gemini**, and the matching supported reader.
3. Process locally, review omissions, and redact sensitive fields.
4. Select destination **Grok**, save the portable library, and verify it.
5. Paste or upload relevant reviewed references where your Grok product supports them.

The tool does not connect Gemini and Grok accounts or recreate native history.
