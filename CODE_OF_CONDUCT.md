# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and evidence, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, spam and disclosure of private data are not welcome. Respect differing experience and accessibility needs. Do not post credentials or sensitive reproductions.

Report concerns privately to hello@huzaifa.io. Maintainers may remove harmful content, request changes, or restrict participation proportionately. Reports should be handled confidentially where possible; no absolute confidentiality promise is made. This original policy applies to project discussions, issues and pull requests.
